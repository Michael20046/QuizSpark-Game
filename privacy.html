Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuizSpark ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares information when you use the QuizSpark website and installable app (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication may process your email address, account identifier, display name and authentication information. If you use Google sign-in, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name, email address and profile image, subject to your Google settings.
  • Game and learning information: We store information associated with your account, such as points, games played, correct answers, completed lessons and lesson quiz progress, to operate your profile, wallet and leaderboard.
  • Reviews: If you submit a review, we store the rating, comment, display name and submission/update timestamps. Reviews may be visible to other visitors.
  • Device storage: QuizSpark may save an unfinished quiz draft in your browser or device storage so you can resume it later. This information remains on that device unless you clear the relevant browser/app data.
  • Usage information: Google Analytics may collect usage and device information, such as pages or features used, interactions, approximate location derived from technical information, device/browser details and identifiers, to help us understand and improve the Service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide quizzes and lessons, calculate and display points, operate the leaderboard and reviews, remember unfinished quizzes on your device, understand Service usage, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and respond to support requests.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

QuizSpark may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners when advertising is enabled. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize ads, subject to applicable law and your choices. Learn more in Google's advertising policies and information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics, advertising partners and service providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies. Your browser settings may allow you to block or delete cookies and clear local website data. Some features may not work as expected if storage is disabled.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with the service providers described above when needed to operate QuizSpark, and may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the Service, or in connection with a business transfer. Your display name, points and submitted reviews may be shown publicly in relevant QuizSpark features.
