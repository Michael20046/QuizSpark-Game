This Privacy Policy explains how QuizSpark ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit or use the QuizSpark website and installable app (the "Service"). Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account and sign-in information: When you register or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes information needed to authenticate your account, which may include your email address, user identifier, display name and authentication records. If you choose Google sign-in, Google may provide profile details such as your name, email address and profile image, depending on your account settings.
  • Quiz and learning activity: QuizSpark stores activity associated with your account to provide features such as your profile, points, games played, correct answers, completed lessons, daily challenges, achievements and leaderboard.
  • Reviews: If you submit a review, we may store its rating, comment, display name and relevant timestamps. Reviews may be visible to other visitors.
  • Information stored on your device: The Service may use browser storage to remember settings or an unfinished quiz. This information is stored on your device and may be removed by clearing the relevant browser or app data.
  • Usage and technical information: Google Analytics, where enabled, may process information about visits and interactions, device and browser details, identifiers and approximate location derived from technical information. This helps us understand how the Service is used and improve it.

Information may be used to create and secure accounts; provide quizzes, stories and lessons; save progress; calculate points and achievements; operate profiles, leaderboards and reviews; understand usage; maintain and improve the Service; investigate abuse or technical problems; and respond to questions and privacy requests.

Do not use a display name or submit a review containing information you do not want other visitors to see.
