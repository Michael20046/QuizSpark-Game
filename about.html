About QuizSpark

Welcome to QuizSpark — a place to learn, play and keep growing.

QuizSpark is a learning and entertainment website that brings original stories, QuizSpark University courses, interactive quizzes and games together in one place. Visitors can read, study, practise and play while building knowledge at their own pace.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning more engaging and approachable. We believe curiosity grows when people have opportunities to ask questions, practise new skills, explore ideas and celebrate progress.

Explore QuizSpark

  • Quiz Arena: Answer questions across topics such as general knowledge, mathematics, English, science, technology, business and life skills. Choose a difficulty and challenge yourself.
  • QuizSpark University: Explore a growing catalogue of courses and subject pathways, from beginner topics to practical skills.
    • \n
  • Learning Centre: Read educational lessons with explanations and examples, then test your understanding with lesson quizzes.
  • Story Library: Explore original stories from different countries and cultures, reading them page by page.
    • \n
  • Games and Quiz Arena: Play interactive quizzes, challenges and learning games across a range of subjects.
  • Daily Challenge: Return for a rotating daily question and build a regular learning habit.
  • Achievements and Ranks: Follow your quiz progress, earn in-app points and unlock achievement badges as you learn.
  • Invite Friends: Share QuizSpark with people who may enjoy learning and playing too.

Learning, Progress and Points

QuizSpark uses quizzes, lesson activities and progress features to encourage practice. Points and badges are in-app progress features; points currently have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. They are not payment or guaranteed prizes.

Our Approach

We aim to keep improving the experience, add useful learning material and make the platform easier to use on mobile devices as well as computers. We welcome thoughtful feedback that helps us make QuizSpark clearer, more useful and more enjoyable.
